GOOD TO KNOW

Curious?
We get it.

A few answers to help you start with confidence.

:)
I am a complete beginner. Can I start here?

Yes. A1 is the starting level of the learning path. Begin with a published A1 course, follow the lessons in order, then practise the vocabulary.

Will I see Bangla explanations?

Wortly supports German lessons and vocabulary with English and Bangla translations. What appears in a specific lesson depends on what has been published in the course CMS.

How do the flashcards and quizzes work?

The learner website creates review cards and short practice quizzes from published vocabulary. They are designed for recall and repetition, not an official proficiency test.

Can I use Wortly on my phone?

Yes. The learner website is designed for mobile, tablet and desktop screens. Your study progress is saved locally in the browser you use.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Not yet. At this stage, completed lessons and word progress are saved in this browser only. Learner accounts and cloud sync can be added in a later release.
