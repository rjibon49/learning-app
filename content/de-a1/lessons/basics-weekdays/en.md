All days of the week are **masculine** (der) and most end in **-tag**.

## The seven days

| Deutsch | English | Short |
|---|---|---|
| der Montag | Monday | Mo. |
| der Dienstag | Tuesday | Di. |
| der Mittwoch | Wednesday | Mi. |
| der Donnerstag | Thursday | Do. |
| der Freitag | Friday | Fr. |
| der Samstag | Saturday | Sa. |
| der Sonntag | Sunday | So. |

In North Germany Saturday is also called **der Sonnabend**.

## on Monday = am Montag

| Deutsch | English |
|---|---|
| am Montag | on Monday |
| montags | on Mondays (every Monday) |
| am Wochenende | at the weekend |
| von Montag bis Freitag | from Monday to Friday |
| heute / morgen / gestern | today / tomorrow / yesterday |
| übermorgen / vorgestern | the day after tomorrow / the day before yesterday |

## Questions

- Welcher Tag ist heute? – Heute ist Mittwoch. — What day is it today? – Today is Wednesday.
- Wann hast du Zeit? – Am Freitag. — When do you have time? – On Friday.

## Opening hours

You will see signs like this on shops and offices:

| Deutsch | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Mo.–Fr. 9–18 Uhr | Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. |
| Sa. 9–14 Uhr | Saturday, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. |
| So. geschlossen | closed on Sunday |

> **Tip:** In Germany most shops are closed on Sunday. Do your shopping on Saturday!
