"First, second, third" are **Ordinalzahlen**. You need them for dates, birthdays and floors.

## How to build them

- 1–19: number + **-te** → zwei → der zweite
- from 20: number + **-ste** → zwanzig → der zwanzigste
- In writing a point shows it is an ordinal: **3.** = dritte

## Learn these four — they are irregular

| Zahl | Deutsch |
|---|---|
| 1. | erste |
| 3. | dritte |
| 7. | siebte |
| 8. | achte |

## Dates

| Deutsch | English |
|---|---|
| Heute ist der 3. Mai. (der dritte Mai) | Today is the 3rd of May. |
| Welches Datum ist heute? | What is the date today? |
| Ich habe am 14. Juni Geburtstag. (am vierzehnten Juni) | My birthday is on 14 June. |
| Wann sind Sie geboren? – Am 2. 9. 1999. | When were you born? – On 2 September 1999. |

- "**der** dritte" → for "it is …"
- "**am** dritten" → for "on …" (the ending becomes **-en**)
- Written: 14.06.2026 → "day", month, year

## Floors

- im ersten Stock — on the first floor (one floor above the ground floor)
- im Erdgeschoss — on the ground floor

> **Tip:** On forms you will see "Geburtsdatum" (date of birth) written as TT.MM.JJJJ — Tag, Monat, Jahr.
