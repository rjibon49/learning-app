The best-known A1 exams are **Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1** and **telc Deutsch A1**. Both test the same four skills. This course follows that format, with original practice tasks.

## The four parts

| Part | What you do | Time (Goethe) |
|---|---|---|
| Hören (listening) | short everyday conversations, announcements, phone messages | about 20 minutes |
| Lesen (reading) | short notes and emails, small ads, signs | 25 minutes |
| Schreiben (writing) | fill in a form, write a short message | 20 minutes |
| Sprechen (speaking) | in a small group: introduce yourself, ask and answer, make requests | about 15 minutes |

Hören, Lesen and Schreiben are done together on one day, one after the other. Sprechen is a **group exam** with usually four candidates.

## Task types you will meet

- **a, b or c?** — choose the right answer.
- **richtig oder falsch?** — is the statement right or wrong?
- **Formular** — fill in missing details.
- **Kurze Nachricht** — write about 30 words.

| Deutsch | English |
|---|---|
| die Prüfung | exam |
| der Prüfungsteil | part of the exam |

| ankreuzen | to tick, mark |

| der Antwortbogen | answer sheet |
| Sie hören den Text zweimal. | You hear the text twice. |

| bestehen / bestanden | to pass / passed |

## Passing

You need about **60 %** of the total points to pass. You don't need to be perfect — you need to be steady across all four parts.

> **Tip:** Check the latest information on the official website of your exam centre before you register — dates, prices and small details can change.
