## Talking about food

- "Zum Frühstück esse ich Brot mit Käse." — For breakfast I eat bread with cheese.
- "Ich esse gern Reis mit Fisch." — I like eating rice with fish.
- "Ich esse kein Schweinefleisch." — I don't eat pork.
- "Ich bin Vegetarier." — I'm a vegetarian.
- "Ich trinke lieber Tee." — I prefer tea.
- "Mein Lieblingsessen ist Biryani." — My favourite food is biryani.

## gern – lieber – am liebsten

| Deutsch | English |
|---|---|
| Ich trinke gern Kaffee. | I like coffee. |
| Ich trinke lieber Tee. | I prefer tea. |
| Ich trinke am liebsten Wasser. | I like water best. |

> **Tip:** "Ich esse kein …" is useful if you don't eat pork or meat. In Germany, many products are labelled "ohne Schwein" or "vegetarisch".
