Colours help you describe clothes, cars and everything around you.

## Light and dark

- **hell** + colour = light: hellblau (light blue), hellgrün
- **dunkel** + colour = dark: dunkelblau (dark blue), dunkelrot

## Asking about colours

| Deutsch | English |
|---|---|
| Welche Farbe hat das Auto? | What colour is the car? |
| Das Auto ist rot. | The car is red. |
| Was ist deine Lieblingsfarbe? | What is your favourite colour? |
| Meine Lieblingsfarbe ist Blau. | My favourite colour is blue. |

## Colour after "ist" — no ending

When the colour comes after the noun with **ist / sind**, it does not change:

- Die Banane ist **gelb**.
- Der Himmel ist **blau**.
- Die Schuhe sind **schwarz**.

(Before a noun colours get endings — "ein rotes Auto". You will learn this later.)

## Colours in the flag

The German flag is **schwarz, rot, gold**. The flag of Bangladesh is **grün und rot**.

> **Tip:** As a noun the colour is written with a capital letter: Ich mag **Blau**. Meine Lieblingsfarbe ist **Grün**.
