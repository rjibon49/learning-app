The months look almost like English. They are all **masculine** (der) and you use **im** with them.

## The twelve months

| Deutsch | English |
|---|---|
| der Januar | January |
| der Februar | February |
| der März | March |
| der April | April |
| der Mai | May |
| der Juni | June |
| der Juli | July |
| der August | August |
| der September | September |
| der Oktober | October |
| der November | November |
| der Dezember | December |

On the phone people say **Juno** and **Julei** so June and July are not mixed up.

## in May = im Mai

| Deutsch | English |
|---|---|
| im Januar | in January |
| Anfang März | at the beginning of March |
| Mitte Juli | in the middle of July |
| Ende Dezember | at the end of December |
| am 1. Mai | on the 1st of May |

## Questions

- In welchem Monat hast du Geburtstag? – Im Oktober. — Which month is your birthday? – In October.
- Wann beginnt der Kurs? – Im September. — When does the course start? – In September.

## Holidays to know

| Deutsch | Date |
|---|---|
| Neujahr | 1 January |
| Tag der Arbeit | 1 May |
| Tag der Deutschen Einheit | 3 October |
| Weihnachten | 24–26 December |
| Silvester | 31 December |

> **Tip:** Remember the three prepositions: **um** + time (um 8 Uhr), **am** + day/date (am Montag, am 3. Mai), **im** + month/season (im Mai, im Sommer).
