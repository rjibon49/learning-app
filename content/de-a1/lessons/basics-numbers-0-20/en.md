You need numbers for prices, phone numbers, ages and the time. Learn 0–12 by heart — the rest is built from them.

## Using numbers

- Ich bin neunzehn Jahre alt. — I am 19 years old.
- Das kostet zwölf Euro. — That costs 12 euros.
- Meine Hausnummer ist sieben. — My house number is 7.

## Phone numbers

Germans say phone numbers digit by digit or in pairs. On the phone, **zwo** is often used instead of zwei so it does not sound like drei:

- 0171 2345 = null, eins, sieben, eins – zwo, drei, vier, fünf

> **Tip:** "eins" becomes "ein" before a noun: ein Kaffee, eine Pizza.
